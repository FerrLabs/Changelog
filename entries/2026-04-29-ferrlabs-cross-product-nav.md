product: 'ferrlabs'

---

The footer of every product site (ferrflow.com, ferrvault.com, ferrtrack.com, ferrgrowth.com) now carries a "More from FerrLabs" block: each sibling product as a row with its accent dot, name, one-line tagline, and link.

ferrlabs.com itself stays the canonical entry point but no longer the only path between products. The brand line at the bottom: "Ferr<Product>: a FerrLabs product": links back to ferrlabs.com.
