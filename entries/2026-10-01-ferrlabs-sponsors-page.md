FerrFlow, FerrFleet Runner, our UI kit and our Rust crates are open source, and they run on CI runners, documentation hosting and domains that someone pays for. FerrLabs now has an Open Collective so the people and companies who rely on them can share that cost, and every euro in and out is public there.

There are five monthly tiers. Coffee, Supporter and Backer put your name on [ferrlabs.com/sponsors](https://ferrlabs.com/sponsors/), and the higher two add a Supporter badge, a monthly letter, early access and a vote on the roadmap. Company Sponsor puts your logo on that page, and Partner adds it to the ferrlabs.com home page with a line about your company. One-off amounts work too.

The sponsors page reads Open Collective directly, so a new contribution shows up there without waiting for a release.
